# Privacy Policy

## What the game stores

`Pill ID` stores a small amount of gameplay data in Devvit Redis:

- submitted leaderboard names
- best scores
- per-question error reports
- aggregate answer-mistake counts used to improve distractor selection

## What is not intended

The game is not intended to collect sensitive personal data. Do not submit:

- legal names
- contact details
- addresses
- medical data
- any other private or identifying information

## Why data is stored

Stored data is used only to:

- show high scores
- help administrators identify broken or ambiguous questions
- improve answer option difficulty over time

## Data scope

Leaderboard and gameplay analytics are stored in global Devvit Redis so they can be shared across the app experience.

## Data retention

Data may be retained for as long as needed to operate, debug, and improve the game, unless manually removed by the administrators.
